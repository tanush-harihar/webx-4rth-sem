It

by Stephen King

A small town called Derry looks normal.
It is not.
It feeds on children.

An ancient entity lives beneath it.
It is older than history.
Older than fear itself.
It awakens every 27 years to hunt.

It does not have one form.
It becomes what you fear most.
Clown. Monster. Ghost. Disease. Death wearing a smile.

Seven children discover the truth.
They are broken. Bullied. Abused. Ignored. Lonely.
But together, they are not weak.

Friendship becomes their weapon.
Belief their shield. Imagination their sword.

The real horror: fear survives when ignored.
Face it, or it waits for you to be alone again.
