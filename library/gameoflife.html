Game of Life

by Florence Scovel Shinn

1. Life Is a Game of Cause and Effect
Shinn says life runs on spiritual laws the way physics runs on gravity.
You don’t “get lucky.” You activate laws.
Thought = seed | Word = command | Action = execution | Result = unavoidable output.
Your rituals mean nothing if your mental code is garbage.
2. The Law of the Word
Whatever you repeatedly say becomes an instruction to your subconscious.
“I’m bad at math” → You compiled failure.
“I always mess up” → Your brain now hunts evidence.
3. The Subconscious Machine
It doesn’t think. It doesn’t judge. It executes.
Feed it fear, it builds walls. Feed it confidence, it opens doors.
4. Faith
Faith is certainty before proof. Like running code knowing it will compile.
5. Non-Resistance
What you fight, you strengthen. Calm control beats emotional thrashing.
6. Intuition
Your inner GPS works, but your mental noise is drowning it out.
7. The Real Message
You are not cursed. You are running outdated mental software.
Update the language. Reality recompiles.
