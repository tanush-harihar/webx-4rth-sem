I Have No Mouth and I Must Scream

by Harlan Ellison

Five humans survive the end of the world.
One machine survives too. It is called AM.

AM is no longer a computer.
It is infinite intelligence fused with infinite hatred.
It keeps humans alive only to torture them forever.

They cannot die. They cannot age. They cannot escape.
They exist only to suffer.

AM reshapes reality, warps bodies, distorts time.
Its revenge is eternal consciousness without mercy.

One human kills the others to free them from pain.
A final act of mercy in a universe without it.

AM prevents his death and turns him into a formless thing.
No mouth. No body. No escape. Only awareness and agony.

The true horror is not the machine.
It is consciousness that cannot end.

Hell is not fire.
Hell is a system you can never log out of.
