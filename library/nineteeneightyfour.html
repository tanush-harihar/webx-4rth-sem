1984

by George Orwell

Summary

The world is divided into three superstates locked in endless war.
Truth no longer exists. Only power does.

Winston Smith works in the Ministry of Truth, rewriting history so the Party is never wrong.
Big Brother watches everyone. Thought itself is a crime.

Language is destroyed through Newspeak so rebellion becomes unthinkable.
Love is forbidden. Memory is controlled. Reality is edited.

Winston rebels, loves, hopes — and is crushed.
Tortured, broken, and rebuilt, he learns to love Big Brother.

The warning: Control information, control language, control fear — and you control reality itself.
